# GloNation
Glonation is a fitness/health business focused on giving our customer all the tools to get them to their dream fitness goal.

**Move. Glow. Belong.**

We are the Entrepreneurship 1 class at CRHS, and our goal with GloNation is to get all our customers to their healthiest version of their self.

## Website
`index.html` is our complete website in a single file: memberships, shop with Virtual Enterprise buy buttons, cart, English/Spanish switch, the Glo Lab workout timer and activity rings, and our contact info.

To publish it, turn on GitHub Pages: **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → main → / (root) → Save**.
